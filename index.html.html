02 — Published Research

Accuracy is not trust.

Published · Zenodo

WiCaP: Calibrated Uncertainty for WiFi Sensing and Human Activity Recognition

A study of when WiFi CSI-based activity recognisers can be trusted, and what happens when they silently fail in new environments.

Problem

WiFi Channel State Information can recognise human activity without cameras. But models that score high in the lab often degrade in new rooms or setups, and still output confident predictions. These silent failures make them risky to deploy.

Approach

I trained a 1D CNN on CSI data, then wrapped it with conformal prediction (APS and RAPS) to produce prediction sets with coverage guarantees. I measured calibration with ECE and used selective prediction to abstain when uncertain, evaluating across environments and out-of-distribution data.

Why it matters: a classifier that is right 98% of the time but can't tell you which 2% is wrong is hard to use in healthcare, safety, or smart-home settings. Calibrated uncertainty lets a system know what it doesn't know.
